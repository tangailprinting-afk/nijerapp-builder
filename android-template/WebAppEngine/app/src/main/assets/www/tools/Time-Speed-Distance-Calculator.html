Key Formulas & Rules
Basic TSD Triangle

Distance = Speed × Time
Speed = Distance ÷ Time
Time = Distance ÷ Speed

Unit Conversion (Golden Rule)

To convert km/h to m/s: Multiply by 5/18
To convert m/s to km/h: Multiply by 18/5

Average Speed

Average Speed = Total Distance ÷ Total Time
(If distances are equal: 2xy / (x+y))

Trains

Crossing a Pole: Distance = Length of Train.
Crossing a Platform: Distance = Length of Train + Length of Platform.
