Educational Formula Guide
Types of Events
Independent Events: The outcome of A does not affect B. P(A ∩ B) = P(A) × P(B)
Mutually Exclusive: A and B cannot happen at the same time. P(A ∩ B) = 0
Addition Rule (Union)
General: P(A ∪ B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A ∩ B)
If Mutually Exclusive: P(A ∪ B) = P(A) + P(B)
Combinatorics
Factorial (n!): Product of all positive integers less than or equal to n. e.g., 4! = 4×3×2×1 = 24.
Permutations (nPr): Ordered arrangements. n! / (n - r)!
Combinations (nCr): Unordered selections. n! / [r! × (n - r)!]
