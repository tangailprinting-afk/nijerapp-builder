Vector Mathematics Guide
Magnitude & Unit Vector
Magnitude |v|: Length of the vector.
|v| = √(x² + y² + z²)
Unit Vector (v̂): A vector of length 1 pointing in the same direction.
v̂ = v / |v|
Dot Product (v · w)
Scalar result measuring how parallel two vectors are.
v · w = (vₓ × wₓ) + (v_y × w_y) + (v_z × w_z)
If v · w = 0, the vectors are Orthogonal (Perpendicular).
Cross Product (v × w)
Returns a vector perpendicular to both v and w (3D only).
Follows the Right-Hand Rule in physics for torque and magnetic force.
Angle Between Vectors
Derived from the dot product formula:
θ = arccos( (v · w) / (|v| |w|) )
